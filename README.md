# Three-leaf counterexamples to Mukherjee and Ray's connected-forcing crossing question

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Using Brimkov's earlier starlike enumeration (May 2017, Proposition 7.11), we answer Mukherjee and Ray's Problem 5.3 in On the connected zero forcing polynomial of finite trees, arXiv:2611.10985v1 (2026), Section 5, p. 21, negatively even for two three-leaf trees and even when their order may be exchanged. For every n at least 10 we construct such a pair: its coefficient differences at sizes 4, 6, n-2 are -2, 1, -1, so neither order admits the requested single crossing. The construction follows from an explicit enumeration of connected forcing sets in a three-armed tree. We also explain the smaller, ordered path-star obstruction from the posers' own examples. For the neighboring unicyclic problem, a complete five-vertex classification shows that a fixed-leaf class can have neither a least nor a greatest polynomial.
